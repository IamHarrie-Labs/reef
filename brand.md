# Reef brand

Direction: quiet, editorial, cream and forest green. A full-viewport landscape
image on the landing page (original, `web/public/reef-hero-landscape.webp`).
The mark is a solid triangle beside the wordmark.

Colors: dark #2d3a2e, green #3d5a3e, light #f5f3ef, cream #faf8f5.
Verdict colors are muted, never alarm-red or celebratory green: Supported is a
soft sage, Unproven a warm sand, Unfavourable a muted clay.
Font: Helvetica Neue Light with Helvetica/Arial fallbacks.

Copy: plain, measured, human. Use em dashes where they help the sentence.
Explain the data's limits without claiming guaranteed returns or live prices.
No invented backers, credit offers, user counts or endorsements.

The landing page leads with one real comparison from the data (headline yield
against what survives cost and risk). The dashboard uses the same palette,
typography, pill buttons and spacing, and reads the corrected v2 evidence.
