# Reef brand

Direction: quiet, editorial, cream and forest green. A landscape
image on the landing page (original, `web/public/reef-hero-landscape.webp`).
The mark is Reef Seal, the approved third logo concept: four organic landforms
separated by flowing channels. The production mark is a crisp, flat SVG at
`web/public/brand/reef-seal.svg`. Pair it with the title-case “Reef” wordmark.
Navigation, the mobile menu and footer use the shared `ReefLogo` component;
the favicon and touch icon use the same seal.

Colors: dark #2d3a2e, green #3d5a3e, light #f5f3ef, cream #faf8f5.
Verdict colors are muted, never alarm-red or celebratory green: Supported is a
soft sage, Unproven a warm sand, Unfavourable a muted clay.
Font: self-hosted Space Grotesk, with Arial/sans-serif fallbacks, regular and medium
weights. No font CDN requests. Main body text is 14–16px; compact research
labels are 12–13px. Use tabular figures for financial comparisons.

Copy: plain, measured, human. Use sentence case for headings, labels and buttons.
Keep instrument symbols, currency codes and established acronyms intact.
Use sentence case and natural punctuation. Do not use em dashes in page copy.
Explain the data's limits without claiming guaranteed returns or live prices.
No invented backers, credit offers, user counts or endorsements.

The landing page pairs a clear research action with one real gross-versus-net
comparison from the dated export. Its subsequent sections explain the workflow,
verdicts and evidence boundaries. The desk uses the same palette and typography,
shared scenario controls, a first-use guide and progressive detail disclosure.
Detailed paper outcomes and on-chain checks belong to the Evidence page.
Use “snapshot research”, not “live desk”, for the exported calculations. A positive
net estimate can still have an unfavourable verdict because risk is assessed
separately. Do not imply that Bitcoin timestamps certify the financial facts.

Research hierarchy: the selected pair’s holding-period estimate, evidence verdict
and plain-language reason follow the scenario controls and precede the watchlist. Show returns
over the selected hold first; annualised estimates are secondary comparisons.
A saved notebook retains its question context until the user explicitly switches
it. Display the next question’s context both in the summary and above the input.
The guided example prepares a question for review, never submits it automatically,
and retains one previous notebook for restoration.

Headings use self-hosted Space Grotesk. Body text and controls use Poppins. Editorial italics use Instrument Serif. Write complete, specific sentences and remove repeated captions. Workflow cards stack with page scrolling. The landing coverage rail automatically scrolls real pairs from the current export on forest green with cream text. The archive artwork is isolated on white, with a curved orbital outline. Hero zoom and ticker motion respect reduced-motion preferences.
