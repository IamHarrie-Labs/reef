# Reef brand

Direction: the supplied Palomar reference, adapted to Reef. Quiet editorial
cream and forest green; a full-viewport landscape video on the landing page.

Colors: dark #2d3a2e, green #3d5a3e, light #f5f3ef, cream #faf8f5.
Font: Helvetica Neue Light with Helvetica/Arial fallbacks. Small supporting
wordmarks use the display fonts from the supplied design brief.

Copy: plain, measured, human. Use em dashes where they help the sentence.
Explain the data's limits without claiming guaranteed returns or live prices.
No invented backers, credit offers, user counts or endorsements.

The landing page has only navigation and a video hero. The dashboard uses the
same palette, typography, pill buttons and spacing. It reads corrected v2 evidence.
