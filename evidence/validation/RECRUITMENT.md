- One experienced perpetual-futures trader who regularly evaluates funding or carry.
- One trader who uses paired positions or leveraged equity products.

Ask community moderators before recruiting there if their rules require it. A builders group can help find introductions, but a developer without trading experience does not meet this test's target-user criteria.

## Copy to send

I’m looking for three experienced perpetual-futures traders to try Reef, a research desk for comparing funding income with trading costs and hedge risk on Bitget RWA pairs.

It’s a 15-minute usability session. You’ll evaluate a hypothetical setup and tell us what is useful or confusing. No trading, wallet connection, account balance or exchange credentials are needed. You don’t need to like the product; critical feedback is the point.

Have you used perpetual futures for at least six months, and do you check funding and execution costs before trading? If so, please reply with your general experience and whether you research paired positions. We’ll arrange a session. Recording is optional and only with your permission.

1. How long have you traded perpetual futures?
2. Describe how you checked funding and execution costs on a recent hypothetical or past setup. Do not share private positions.
3. Have you previously used a hedge or paired position?
4. Have you seen or helped build Reef?

Record P1/P2/P3 rather than names in the public results. Keep contact details separate and private. Do not publish quotes or recordings without the participant's agreement.
